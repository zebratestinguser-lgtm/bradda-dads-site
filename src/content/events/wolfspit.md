The Wolf's Pit Fell Race (sometimes written Wolfspit) climbs from Shatton to the summit of Shatton Moor before descending to the Wolf's Pit above the hamlet of Abney. The first race was on 22 March 2009. It has been cancelled once for snow, and in 2020 and 2021 because of Covid.

- 9 km / 5.6 miles, 450 m / 1,476 ft of climb, FRA category AS
- Minimum age 18
- No dogs on the field (ewes in lamb)

**Course records:** Male: F. Grant, 0:37:56 (2022). Female: O. Walwyn Bush, 0:43:57 (2012).
