The Wolf's Pit Fell Race climbs from Shatton to the summit of Shatton Moor before descending to the Wolf's Pit above the hamlet of Abney. The first race was on 22 March 2009. It has been cancelled once for snow, and in 2020 and 2021 because of Covid.

**Race details (2026):**

- 9 km / 5.6 miles, 450 m / 1,476 ft of climb, FRA category AS
- Start 11:00, car parking from 09:30, Shatton, near Bamford, Hope Valley (grid reference 196825)
- Minimum age 18
- Entry via Fabian4
- No dogs on the field (ewes in lamb)

**Course records:** Male: F. Grant, 0:37:56 (2022). Female: O. Walwyn Bush, 0:43:57 (2012).
