Every summer holidays we take over Town Bottom Playing Field for a family fun day. There are large bouncy castles, games on the park (tin can alley, skittles, dominoes and more) and a barbecue run by the dads, plus other activities that change from year to year, sometimes including a water fight against the dads.

Recent fun days were on 1 August 2023, 30 July 2024 and 27 July 2026 (2025's was cancelled because of the weather): see [the 2026 thank-you](../../news/2026-07-28-fb-a-huge-thank-you-to-everyone-who-helped-make-yesterday-s-bra/).

Earlier fun days were in June: 10 June 2017 at Town Bottom and [15 June 2019 behind the Shoulder of Mutton](../../news/2019-05-17-family-fun-day-2019/).
