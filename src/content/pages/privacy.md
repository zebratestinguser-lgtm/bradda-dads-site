## Who we are

This website is run by Bradda Dads, a volunteer group raising money for Bradwell, Derbyshire.

## No tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

## Payments

Donations and payments are taken by SumUp. When you pay, you're on SumUp's website and their privacy policy applies. We receive the payment details SumUp shares with sellers (such as your name and the amount) so we can keep our accounts.

## The map

The "where the money goes" map loads map images from OpenStreetMap's servers when you scroll to it, so your browser contacts OpenStreetMap. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

## Photos, news and race results

The site includes photos and stories from Bradda Dads events going back to 2001 (many brought across from our old websites), and fell race results that list runners' names, clubs, categories and times as published at the time.

If you'd like a photo, a mention of you or your child, or your race result removed, [contact us](../contact/) and we'll take it down.
