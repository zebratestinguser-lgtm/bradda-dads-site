Each year, Bradda Dads (and families) take part in the Bradwell carnival procession. It’s always a great laugh getting dressed up and learning the dance routines, so if you fancy joining us, please contact one of the Dads or feel free to come along to any of our monthly meetings (on the third Wednesday of the month, at 8:30pm in one of the local pubs, usually the Shoulder).
