FREE ENTRY • Everyone welcome! 🎆

🚨 Final Food & Drink Update! 🚨  
Sadly, Sunshine Pizza can no longer attend this year-  a real shame, but these things happen!

The good news is our Coffee Van is still confirmed ☕🎉  
They’ll be serving hot drinks and delicious cakes (with options for different diets) in the Bonfire Field, so you can stay warm and enjoy a sweet treat while you watch the fun. 🍰

---

🎇 Event Schedule & Timings  
🕟 Torchlit Procession Registration: 4:30–5:00pm at The Brook Café (which will be open)  
🎶 Procession Sets Off: 5:15pm (led by the Queens and Castleton Band)  
🔥 Bonfire Lit: 6:00pm  
💥 Fireworks: 6:30pm

---

⚠️ Please note: No sparklers allowed for everyone’s safety and insurance reasons.
