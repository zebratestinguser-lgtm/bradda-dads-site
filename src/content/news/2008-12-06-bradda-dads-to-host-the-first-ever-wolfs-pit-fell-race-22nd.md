**********NEW FOR 2009**********

The race, which will start at 1030hrs from Townfield Lane, Shatton (opposite High Peak Garden Centre), climbs to the summit of Shatton Moor before descending to the Wolf''s Pit above the hamlet of Abney. At 5.2 miles and 1,424ft of climb, the race is categorised by the Fell Running Association as category A (over 250ft of climb per mile). It would be great to see entries from Bradwell runners. A £5 entry fee is payable on the day and the proceeds of the race to go to Bradda Dads for the benefit of the residents of Bradwell. A web site providing full details of the fell race is in progress, so keep checking [the old race website (archived copy)](https://web.archive.org/web/20090119033122/http://www.wolfspitfellrace.org.uk/) for further information.
