Next Bradda Dads meeting on 19th April at the Bowling Green.  By the way, anyone with a son or daughter at the Infant or Junior school (or surely anyone who wants to get involved) - you are free to come along and get involved. It's all good fun being a part of the organistation, and you get to meet lots of like-minded people who live in and around Bradwell.
