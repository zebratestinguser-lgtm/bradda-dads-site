Another lovely (non-rainy) day and a great turn out for the race. Thanks to everyone for coming to the race and helping to make it another success.  We hope you enjoyed the run, especially the run back up the hill after heading off down Shatton Lane! The pictures were taken at the marshall point just before you headed off down Shatton Lane.  
All the help on the day is voluntary, and all the funds raised go to supporting the Bradwell and Shatton communities.  
Hope to see you again in 2017.
