Bradda Dads helped at the opening of the new Multi-use Games Area (MUGA) by erecting a marquee and gazebos, sponsoring the ladies netball competition and running the barbecue. The event was opened by Paul Jenner from High Peak Radio and attended by Derbyshire Dales District Council and Lightmain; the main contractor. The 'Dads entered a team in the football competition losing narrowly by 1 goal to nil. The basketball competition was won by Bradda Dad Jonathan Morgan!
