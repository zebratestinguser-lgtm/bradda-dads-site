Lots and lots of photos before the race, during the race , at marshall points, and at the end. What a brilliant day - beautiful weather (unless you're a runner perhaps!), lots of smiles, great memories. Thanks everyone for coming along to run the Half or Long Tour - we hope you enjoyed yourselves as much as we did looking after you. You're all an inspiration to me and the others to get out there and keep fit and healthy. Well done, and if you liked the day please tell your friends and bring them along. Feel free to tag yourself on the photos, and hope to see you all again in 2017.  
Lots of brilliant photos on this site too: http://runfurther.com/long-tour-of-bradwell-2016/
