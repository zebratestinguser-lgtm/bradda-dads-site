Very productive meeting again at the Bowling Green. Everyone is gearing up for the Pensioner's Lunch, and the Duck Race tickets are ready for sale. We agreed a donation to FOBS (Friends of Bradwell schools) to meet the cost of party food and gifts for their Christmas party, to Teddy Bears for a baby changing facility, and to Bradwell Sports Club for the cost of a replacement battery for their defibrillator (based at the MUGA). Big thanks to Pete for his chairmanship over the past year, and thanks to Darren (Chair), Stu (Secretary) and Steve (Treasurer) and all the Dads for their continued support and enthusiasm.
