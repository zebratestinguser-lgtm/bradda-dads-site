What an incredible night! The torchlit procession, bonfire, and fireworks were absolutely fantastic. It was brilliant to see so many people come out to enjoy it together.

A huge thank you to everyone who helped make it happen:  
☕ The Brook Café for hosting the procession registration and keeping everyone fuelled  
🎶 Castleton Band for leading the parade so brilliantly  
👑 The Queens for keeping a great local tradition alive  
🎃 Elsie for designing the amazing pumpkin rope fire display  
🚚 The Coffee Van for keeping everyone warm and happy with drinks and cakes  
💥 Breedon for their generous donation towards the fireworks  
🔥 And of course all the Bradda Dads who marshalled traffic, helped set up the field, ensured safety, and ran the fireworks

We are also hugely grateful to everyone who came along and donated so generously. Every penny goes straight back into supporting community events here in Bradwell ❤️

This year’s bonfire was organised entirely by just ten dads. It was hard work but hugely rewarding. Seeing families enjoy the night, the fire, and the fireworks made it all worthwhile.

We would love to have more dads join us. Being a Bradda Dad is about having fun, meeting other dads, and seeing your kids’ faces light up. There’s no minimum commitment, any help is appreciated, whether it’s an hour, one event, or regular involvement. There’s a role for every skill, from planning events to helping on the day or lending a hand wherever needed.

Beyond helping out, it’s a chance to make friends, enjoy teamwork, and take pride in doing something positive for the village and your family. You’ll see the tangible impact of your efforts through events like the Boxing Day Duck Race, Pensioners Lunch, Wolf Pit Fell Race, Gala Day, Family Fun Day, and practical projects like replacing a bridge.

If you’re a dad and fancy helping out, or just want to find out more, drop us a message or comment below. The more of us there are, the easier and more fun it is for everyone!

Thanks again, Bradwell. What a night! 🔥🎆
