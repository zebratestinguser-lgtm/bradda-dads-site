A huge thank you to everyone who took part in this year's Wolf's Pit Fell Race. The turn out was close to the highest number of entrants we have ever had. The weather caused the field to be a little tricky underfoot (and wheels!) and some interesting driving techniques were required to get onto the field. The results will be posted soon on the Wolf's Pit Fell Race web site. See link on this blog.

Some photos contributed by June and Vic Poke
