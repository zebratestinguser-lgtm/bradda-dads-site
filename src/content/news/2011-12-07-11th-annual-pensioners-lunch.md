About 126 Bradwell pensioners came to this year's lunch at the Methodist Hall. Thanks must go to our wives who did a great job in the kitchen, to Stanley Bradwell and the Methodist Hall and to Jo's Pantry for the food. Children from the infant and junior schools visited to sing Christmas songs providing a lovely start to Christmas. Thanks also to James at the Whitehart for gaining brewery sponsorship towards the cost of the free bar.
