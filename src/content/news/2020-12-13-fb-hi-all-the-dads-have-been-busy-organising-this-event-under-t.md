Hi all - the Dads have been busy organising this event under the current restrictions. The idea is to bring some cheer to this difficult year by transporting Father Xmas around the village, while people clap, wave, make a racket and raise a glass in their doorways, windows and gardens. We appreciate some people may not get a good view from their house and would prefer to come out -- please do so, but observe social distancing :-)

Much as we would love to have the streets filled with people, this is not the year for large gatherings -- so please can we ask that you don't share this widely on social media outside the village.

See you next Saturday, Bradwell!
