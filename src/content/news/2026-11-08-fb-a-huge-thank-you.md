We'd like to say a massive thank you to everyone who came along to enjoy our annual Bonfire and Fireworks Night on Saturday. It was fantastic to see so many people come together and enjoy the evening.

Thanks to your generosity, we've raised over £1,000 in donations! Every single penny will go straight towards helping us put on future events and support our village and local community. We are so grateful for your support.

A big thank you also goes to all the dads who put in the hard work behind the scenes to make the evening happen. There's plenty of planning, preparation and work involved, but seeing the smiles, excitement and enjoyment it brings to the village makes it all worthwhile.

Fancy getting involved?

We're always looking for new faces and fresh ideas. If you'd like to lend a hand on the day, or simply find out what we're all about, we'd love to hear from you.

Our next meeting is Tuesday 17th November at 8:30pm, at one of the village pubs (venue to be confirmed). Keep an eye on our Facebook page for the venue update.

There's no obligation, just come along, introduce yourself and see what we're about. Everyone's welcome!

Thanks again, Bradwell. You're what makes it all worthwhile! ❤️
