What a night! Around 600 people came along to this year's village bonfire and fireworks, the weather was kind to us, and the feedback, in person and online, has been brilliant.

The donation table and the LED light-up batons were a big hit. Thank you to everyone who chipped in: every penny goes back into Bradwell.

A huge thank you to **Breedon** for their generous support, which makes the fireworks possible, and to everyone who helped build, marshal, light, serve and tidy up. Every part played, however small, makes a difference.

Already planning for next year? So are we. If you'd like to help, [drop us a line](../../get-involved/).
