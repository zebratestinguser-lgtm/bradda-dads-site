On Thursday 17th Jan 2019, eleven intrepid Bradda Dads risked life and limb in the gloomy depths of Bagshawe Cavern. With tales of Thai football teams ringing in our ears, and hunched over like a troop of geriatric Neanderthals with bad backs, we managed to crawl and slither our way through the dark to eventually reach the stream that feeds Bradwell Brook. Along the way, we were lucky to survive treacherous waters of up to 40cm deep in places, and even though we didn’t find any of the lost plastic ducks, it was an enlightening adventure into the parts of Bradwell you don’t usually see. Thanks to John for organising the trip and the guys at Castleton Hollowford Centre for leading the way.

Note to self: must remember to turn off head torch when having a photo taken in a cave!
