One shared card, delivered to every home in Bradwell. It saves paper and raises money for village events. This year's artwork is by pupils at Bradwell Schools.  
Two ways to get your family's names on it, £5 per family: ✉️ On paper, as usual: fill in the slip in the Bradwell News, pop it in an envelope with £5 and drop it at Bradwell Post Office. 💻 NEW this year, online, through [our Christmas card page](../../christmas-card/). When you check out, please use the "message to the merchant" box to tell us exactly what you'd like in the card (names or message, max 50 characters). Without it we won't know what to print!  
Deadline: Friday 20 November.
