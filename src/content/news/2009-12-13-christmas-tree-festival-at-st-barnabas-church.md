Bradda Dads were one of various community organisations that were invited by St Barnabas Church to take part in a Christmas Tree Festival. The festival was in aid of the fabric fund for roof work and building maintenance. The idea was for each organisation to decorate a Christmas tree to represent what they do in the village. Special thanks to the Ojari family who temporarily gave up this year's real tree for the festival. All the trees looked great with their individual decorations and lights.
