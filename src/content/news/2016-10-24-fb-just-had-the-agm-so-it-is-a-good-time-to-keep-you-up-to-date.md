Just had the AGM so it is a good time to keep you up-to-date with some of the things we have done this year and the donations we have made:  
Organised and paid for village bonfire and firework display / school Guy competition (including St John's ambulance fee)  
Organised and ran Wolf's Pit fell race  
Organised and ran x2 Duck Races (this includes removing weed from the brook)  
Organised and paid for  / distributed annual Bradwell Xmas card  
Helped to organise and run the Long / Short Tours of Bradwell ultra / fell races  
Erected marquees in the church gardens / Glenbrook guides centre.

Examples of 2016 donations:  
Friend of Bradwell (Junior / Infants) School: £1285  
British Legion Poppy appeal wreath: £17  
Bradwell Centenary Players (ice-cream for all children): £68  
Bradwell pre-school: £164  
Bradwell Junior School carpets: £508  
Bradwell Bowl's Club benches: £420  
Hope Valley Young Farmers: £100  
Bradwell Guides (London Eye experience): £282
