As I’m sure you are all aware, we have had to cancel this years race due to the corona virus outbreak. We have only had to cancel once before, due to snow, and lost over £800 in committed expenses. For this reason we put a notice on Fabian4 and the FRA website that refunds would not be available in the event of cancellation. As the notice was put in to cover our committed expenses we have arranged with Fabian4 to offer you the options below.

Bradda Dads are essentially a bunch of blokes that do things for our community, none of us gain materially from any of our fund-raising or events. We’ve organised dozens of fell races and look forward to welcoming you to races in the future, for now though, we hope we can rely on your understanding and generosity. Thank-you.

We are offering all entrants 2 options:

A/ Donate your entry fee to Bradda Dads. We will use your donation to put towards good causes in Bradwell such as the schools, supporting other village groups and organising village events such as the Bonfire or Pensioners Xmas lunch (details of our activities are on this web site). In this case no action is required and we thank you very much in anticipation.

OR

B/ You can claim a refund minus committed costs. Unfortunately our committed costs equate to 72.8% of the £8 entry fee, so we are only able to offer a 27.2% refund, which equates to £2.18 per runner. The refund must be claimed before midnight on 31st March 2020. To claim this you need to access your Fabian4 entry details. If you no longer have the email that gives you access then re-request it from [http://www.fabian4.co.uk/competitor/viewentry.aspx](http://www.fabian4.co.uk/competitor/viewentry.aspx). Once on your entry details page, scroll down to the Cancellation Section at the bottom, and click the “Confirm Cancellation” button.>>.

Please email wolfspitfellrace@gmail.com if you would like to contact us.
