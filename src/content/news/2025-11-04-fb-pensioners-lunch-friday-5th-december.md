Our annual Pensioners’ Lunch is still open for guests and we’d love to see as many familiar faces as possible.

Starting at 11am with coffee, followed by a delicious lunch, great company and plenty of laughter.

We’re hoping for some live music to add to the festive fun, along with performances from the school children and the always entertaining Bradda Dads. 🌟

The lunch is completely free for any pensioner in the village.

If you are physically unable to attend in person due to illness, injury or mobility issues, we’ll make sure a lunch is delivered to your home on the day so you don’t miss out. To book your place, please pop in and see Rob at the Post Office.

Let’s make this year’s lunch another wonderful day full of smiles, songs and festive cheer. 🎅✨
