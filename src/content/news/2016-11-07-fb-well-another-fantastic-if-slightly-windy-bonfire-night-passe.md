Well, another fantastic, if slightly windy bonfire night passed off without too many incidents. Guyclops and Miss Piggy were sacrificed to the flames, and despite the intermittant showers of red hot embers, everyone looked like they were having a great time.    Liz : I hope your eye is OK!  
The Big Bang fireworks guys did us proud again, and some of the Bradda Dads bravely stayed after everyone had gone home to guard against the fire spreading, drain the traditional post-bonfire bottle of whiskey (cheers Darren Rug, Pete and Martin for the 'refreshments'), and to discuss the intricacies of English grammar, in particularly a stimulating debate on the use of possessive apostrophes.  
Well done to everyone for doing a great job of the firewood collection and the organisation on the evening. Thanks for all the donations for the firewood collection and on the night itself. The money raised will now circulate around the village starting with the Pensioners Lunch on Friday 9th December.  
Great to see so many people out supporting this event, and look forward to seeing you again in 2017.
