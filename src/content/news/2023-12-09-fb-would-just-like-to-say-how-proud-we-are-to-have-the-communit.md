Would just like to say how proud we are to have the community we do. Hosting the pentioners lunch yesterday in the Bradwell War Memorial Hall worked really well.

We have seen several posts of gratuity, to which I for one feel very proud to help continue the traditions that began many years ago.

Thankyou to Daniel Plevey for being our main organiser. And all the mums and dads who volunteer their time regularly.

Anyone looking forward to our Boxing Day duck race, with refreshments from Friends of Bradwell Schools?
