Bradda Dads are honoured to receive an award from Derbyshire County Council under the category of 'improving life.' The award is in recognition of fund raising activities and work done for the benefit of the community of Bradwell by Bradda Dads' members, both past and present.

Council Leader Andrew Lewer and Councillor George Wharmby and ten representatives of Bradda Dads
