Latest donations of your money went to fund an equipment storing shed for the pre-school, new goals for the Junior foobal club, and a contribution to buy costumes for the Bradwell Centenary Players pantomine and icecreams for the children who took part in the pantomine. In December 2016 we donated money to the Friends of Bradwell Schools for their Xmas parties, and to the Teddy Bear club for them to buy new toys and a baby changer. We continue to circulate all money raised back into good causes in the village - thanks for your continued support.
