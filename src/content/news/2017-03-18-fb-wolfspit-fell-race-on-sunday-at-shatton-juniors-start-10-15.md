Wolfspit Fell Race on Sunday at Shatton: Juniors start 10:15 with the under-10s fun run followed by under-12s, under-14s and under-16s. The senior race starts at 11AM. Lots of the Hope Valley Hurricanes children will be at the races, so please encourage your children to come and have a go. Or why not go along to Hope Valley Hurricanes children's running club - every Thursday at 6PM: meet at Hope Sports Pavillion (opposite Hope Valley College). It's a fantastic club, the kids love getting out into the fields and fells and they can increase their fitness and make friends at the same time. Please come along - have a walk to Shatton Lane and see all the runners scramble up the hill towards the mast  - a brilliant sight.  
Bradda Dads: wakey, wakey! Saturday 9AM in Malcolm's field for the set-up and then back on Sunday morning at 8:30.
