A huge thank you to everyone who helped make yesterday's Bradda Dads Family Fun Day such a fantastic success! 🐤🦆

Special thanks to Lynne and Nick  at the Shoulder of Mutton for kindly providing the electricity to keep the bouncy castles running all day, Lisa and Sophia at the Café for working so hard serving over 170 ice creams paid for by the Bradda Dads, and Bramhalls for supplying the brilliant bouncy castles.

A massive thank you as well to all the Bradda Dads who gave up their time before, during and after the event to help set everything up, keep it running smoothly and pack everything away.

We'd also like to mention catering. As a small charity, we do our best to make our events inclusive and provided gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan options yesterday. If you have other dietary requirements or allergies, we're more than happy to accommodate wherever we can - we simply ask that you let us know in advance if you're planning to attend. That way we can make sure there's something suitable for everyone, while also avoiding unnecessary food waste and keeping costs down.

Finally, thank you to everyone who came along, supported the day and helped create such a brilliant atmosphere. It was fantastic to see so many families enjoying themselves, and we hope you all had a great day. We look forward to seeing you at the next one!
