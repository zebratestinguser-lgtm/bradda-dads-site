🔥🎆 SAVE THE DATE 🎆🔥 Bradda Dads Bonfire & Fireworks is on Saturday 7 November in the usual field off Micklow Lane.  
👉 Event details: https://www.facebook.com/events/2003555433680578  
Food from 5:30pm, bonfire lit at 6pm, fireworks at 6:30pm, then the after-show party at The Bowling Green. Free entry. Hit "Going" on the event and share with friends!  
💛 Entry is free. Donations are gratefully accepted on the night or online at https://bradda-dads-community-group.sumupstore.com/ Every penny goes back into the community of Bradwell.
