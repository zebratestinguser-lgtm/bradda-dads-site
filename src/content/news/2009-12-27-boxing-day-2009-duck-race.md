The annual Boxing Day duck race was made more festive by the remains of the recent heavy snow fall. Despite being slippery under foot a crowd of local people and visitors watched the craft race and then 500 ducks as they bobbed their way down the brook. There were prizes for the craft race winner and for the best-designed craft in each year group. The duck race results will be posted here later. Thanks to all who supported this year's race.
