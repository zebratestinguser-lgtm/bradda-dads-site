FREE ENTRY • Fun for all the family 🎆

🚨 Food Update! 🚨  
Our usual burger van can’t make it (date mix-up, no fault of theirs!) - but Sunshine Pizza has kindly stepped up to help us out. We’re so grateful to them for making sure everyone can enjoy a warm meal! 🍕✨

In the Bonfire Field, they’ll be serving fresh wood-fired pizzas,  including vegetarian and gluten-free options. We suggest bringing cash just in case the card machines struggle! 💡

Enjoy a slice under the fireworks, or stick around after for a cosy supper by the fire, or grab a takeaway pizza for home. Let’s support our amazing local suppliers! ❤️

☕ Coffee Van also on site – hot drinks & cakes to keep you warm!

🎇 Evening Schedule  
🕟 Torchlit Procession Registration: 4:30–5:00pm (The Brook Café open)  
🎶 Procession Sets Off: 5:15pm  
🔥 Bonfire Lit: 6:00pm  
💥 Fireworks: 6:30pm

⚠️ No sparklers, please — for everyone’s safety.
