Thanks so much to everyone who’s offered to help, but we’re pleased to say we’ve got more than enough wood stockpiled for this year’s bonfire, more than we can burn on the night!

So please:  
❌ Don’t drop any wood, pallets or garden waste off at the bonfire field  
❌ Be advised, the Bradda Dads are not collecting.

Anything left on the field just has to be cleared away by our volunteers, so we’d really appreciate your help keeping it clear.

We can’t wait to see you all on the night! 🎆

The Bradda Dads
