124 of Bradwell's finest pensioners, plus one very suspect unidentified 'lady' (see if you can identify her? from the photos), had lunch served by Bradda Dads assisted by a number of their partners. Thanks to all concerned, including Stanley Bradwell for the use of the Methodist Hall, Bakewell and Eyam Community Transport and Jo's Pantry for the catering (which was excellent), James at the White Hart and the children from Bradwell Junior School who sang for everyone.
