In the late evening of Sunday 4th November 2012 the bonfire built the day before was torched by an arsonist(s). The show must go on and a replacement fire is set to be built in time for Friday's event. High Peak Radio have done a great job of publicising that the bonfire is to go ahead as normal.

Below are some photos from the 12th annual event held on Friday 9 November. A bumper crowd turned up and thanks to everyone for their generous donations. Hot dogs, soup, tea, coffee and cold drinks were served. Bradda Dads hope you enjoyed the show and Big Bang once again provided a great firework display. Even the drizzle didn't dampen the community spirit. A huge thanks to Reg Davies who helped members of Bradda Dads, past and present, to build the replacement fire and also to staff at [Networks](http://www.networks3d.net/web%20pages/networks_contact.html).
