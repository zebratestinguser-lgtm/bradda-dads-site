We have made the decision to run the ball race this Boxing Day instead of the duck race.

We feel it wouldn’t be very health and safety conscious to stand in the brook, let alone try and catch the ducks…..

We have enough marshals, but would appreciate extra help on the corners to guide the balls.

Still running the same times, maybe add wheels to the crazy rafts / create a crazy sleigh to be sent down from the white hart, to the phone box.

I’ve attached the original duck race poster for the timings, ticket sales and refreshments will be available at town bottom park as usual.

Thank you for your support.
