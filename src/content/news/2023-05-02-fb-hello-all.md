I would like to thank all the people who have assisted me with the organisation of the Bradwell Hillbilly cycle sportive for the last few years.

Especially the Bradda Dads and Pre-school staff/parents. Last year we made just over £2000. Split between pre-school and the Bradda Dads (who used the money to fund the pensioners Christmas lunch).

This year’s event is being held on Sunday the 18th June.

On the day we will need help marshalling and registering riders.

We are also trying to promote the event as much as possible.

Since covid we have had a drop in riders. Only 90 last year, down from 130.

Every extra rider is another £28 for worthy causes. Please help us any way you can to spread the word.

Cheers Dan
