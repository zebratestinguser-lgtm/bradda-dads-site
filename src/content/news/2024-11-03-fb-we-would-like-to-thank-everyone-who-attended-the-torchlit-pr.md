We would like to thank everyone who attended the torchlit procession, and the fireworks event. And the kids who did some lovely drawings, that we got to illuminate 😊

Without your bradda dad’s Christmas card and duck race support. (Including business sponsorship) these events wouldn’t be possible.

We also require support from other dads within the area, to help keep these unique events possible.

We raised for  £511.35 of which we will be donating £200 to F.O.B.S

Please keep buying the duck race tickets, and Christmas cards to support your village.

Photos in the comments will be greatly appreciated, even videos. One of the ducklings would like to make a montage. There will be a bucket in the post office next week for participation prizes for the kids 😊
