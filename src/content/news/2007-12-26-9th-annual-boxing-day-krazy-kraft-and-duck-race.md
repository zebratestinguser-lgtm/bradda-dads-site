As 2007 draws to a close, Bradda Dads complete their year of events with the 9th annual Boxing Day krazy kraft and duck races. In the Krazy Kraft race there were prizes for the best-designed krafts in the under eights and the eights and over, and the under-eights winner's kraft went on to win the race too. 500 yellow plastic ducks (plus a couple of ducks on steroids) then bobbed their way down the brook and the first duck home was number 337 and joint first prizes went to Derek White and Mr Hawkins.
