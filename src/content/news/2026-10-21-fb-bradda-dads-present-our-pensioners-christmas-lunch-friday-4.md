🎄 Bradda Dads present our Pensioners' Christmas Lunch: Friday 4 December at the Memorial Hall.  
👉 Event details: https://www.facebook.com/events/2356090308535467  
A free festive celebration for pensioners and retirees living in the village: a hot lunch, complimentary drinks, and performances from local schools and the famous Bradda Dads' choir. Doors open 11am, lunch at 12:30pm.  
To book, return the RSVP slip to Bradwell Post Office by Friday 20 November. Please share with parents, grandparents and neighbours, especially anyone not on Facebook.  
💛 The lunch is free for our guests. Donations towards it are gratefully accepted online at https://bradda-dads-community-group.sumupstore.com/ Every penny goes back into the community of Bradwell.
