Next Bradda Dads meeting at the Shoulder of Mutton on Friday 9th June from 7PM. Please bring along wives / partners  / children and have a think about ideas for this year's  carnival float. As ever, we are always after new members, so if you have a child / children at school in Bradwell, please consider joining the group. We meet once a month (on the third Wednesday of the month in one of the pubs), and any activities we organise are shared out within the group so there isn't a massive time commitment. You also get the opportunity to help out other community fund raising organisations (e.g. Bradwell Hillbilly on Sunday 25th June), meet and get to know lots of like minded people in the village, and you can be proud that all the money you help to raise goes back to organisations in your village. You may have noticed that the next meeting is on a Friday (not a Wednesday) - this is because we need to meet to share ideas / prepare for the carnival.
