On a cold but sunny Saturday morning, Malcolm Sowerby, John Boyle, Keith Cooper, Mick Taylor, Mike and Viv Thompson (and Joe!), Jonathan and Caroline Morgan, Jaak Ojari and Mark Johnston walked the 5 mile route of the Wolf's Pit Fell Race. In addition to some very steep climbs, the recent and heaviest snow fall in years made the going tough in places, but we were rewarded with some spectacularly snowy views.
