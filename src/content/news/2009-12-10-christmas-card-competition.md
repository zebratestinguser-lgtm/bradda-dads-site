Bradwell Infant and Junior schools entered the festive spirit and took part in the design a Christmas card competition. The winning designs featured on a bespoke christmas card that was given to every pensioner who attended the Bradda Dads lunch at the Methodist Hall and all the children's designs were displayed as well.

A winner was picked from each school, judged by Bradda Dad Neal Seymour. Well done to all the other children - a marvellous effort. Both schools received cash prizes from Bradda Dads and Headteacher of the Infant School, Geoff Wharton, is pictured receiving his school's cheque.
