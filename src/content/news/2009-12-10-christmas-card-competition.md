Bradwell Infant and Junior schools entered the festive spirit and took part in the design a christmas card competition. The winning designs featured on a bespoke christmas card that was given to every pensioner who attended the Bradda Dads lunch at the Methodist Hall and all the children's designs were displayed as well.

Infant School Lauren Harrison's picture was judged the winner by Bradda Dad Neal Seymour. Megan Pembleton was the winner from the Junior School. Well done to all the other children - a marvellous effort. Both schools received cash prizes from Bradda Dads and Headteacher of the Infant School, Geoff Wharton, is pictured receiving his school's cheque.
