A brilliant evening for all the family - with food, music, a torchlit procession, bonfire and fireworks 🎆

🍔 Food & Drinks (from 4pm in the spectator field)

Burgers, Hotdogs, Bacon & Egg Butties - from £6

Whopper Burger (double burger, bacon, cheese & onions) - £8.50

🔥 Bonfire Lit: 6:00pm
