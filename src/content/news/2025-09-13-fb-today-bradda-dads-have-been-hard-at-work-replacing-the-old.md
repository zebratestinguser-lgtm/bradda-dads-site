Today, Bradda Dads have been hard at work replacing the old footbridge between Hall Gate View and Springfield Road. We would like to thank Bradwell Parish Council for their grant contribution towards the costs of this much-needed replacement.

Please note that while the new bridge is safe to use, it is not yet fully completed. The middle rails are still to be installed, so we advise extra care when crossing—particularly with young children or pets.

Work is scheduled to continue tomorrow (weather permitting), during which time the route will be closed.
