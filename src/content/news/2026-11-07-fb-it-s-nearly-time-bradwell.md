Wrap up warm, bring the family and come and join us for a great evening.  
📍 Micklow Lane field, behind the Bowling Green. Access on foot from Beggars Plot playing field, before Smalldale.  
🚫 Please remember: strictly no sparklers in the field. Our insurance does not cover them.  
💛 Free entry, no tickets needed. Donations are very welcome, with every penny going straight back into our community.  
See you later, Bradwell! 🔥🎆
