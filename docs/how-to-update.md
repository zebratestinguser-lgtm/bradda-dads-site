# How to update the Bradda Dads website

You can add news, photo albums and event details yourself. You can't break the layout or design: the editor only lets you change words and photos.

## Getting in

1. Look for an email invitation to **Pages CMS** and accept it. You don't need a GitHub account.
2. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org** and sign in with the same email address.
3. Choose **bradda-dads-site**. The menu on the left lists everything you can edit.

When you press **Save**, the website updates by itself in about **2 to 3 minutes**. Refresh the page to see your change.

## Add a news story

1. Click **News**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Headline**
   - **Date posted**
   - **Topic** (Bonfire, Duck race and so on)
   - **Summary:** one or two sentences, shown on the news cards.
3. **Photo** is optional.
   - If you add one, fill in **Photo description**: say what's in the photo, for people who can't see it.
   - If you don't add one, the story gets a coloured tile with the Bradda Dads duck instead.
4. Write the story in the **Story** box.
5. Press **Save**.

The newest story is always the big one on the homepage.

## Add a photo album

1. Click **Photo albums**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Album name**, e.g. "Bonfire 2026".
   - **Date the photos were taken**
   - **Category**
3. Under **Photos**, click **Add** for each photo.
   - Upload it straight from your phone or computer. Big photos are shrunk automatically.
   - Give each one a **Description**, e.g. "Dads in hi-vis building the bonfire".
4. You can link the album to one of the yearly events (**Event**), so it appears on that event's page.
5. Press **Save**.

To link an album to a news story, open the story and pick the album under **Photo album**.

To see how an album looks on the website, open it on the live site: https://zebratestinguser-lgtm.github.io/bradda-dads-site/photos/ (every album is listed there). Links inside the editor that open GitHub are for checking files only, and you can ignore them.

## Update an event

1. Click **Events** and open the event.
2. Change what you need. Things that change each year:
   - **Status:** "open" while tickets or entries are on sale, "done" once it's over.
   - **Date and start time**
   - **Start time confirmed:** tick it once the time is certain.
   - **Entries or sales close:** "Open now" changes to "Entries closed" automatically after this time.
   - **Main button link:** usually the SumUp link.
3. Press **Save**.

## Add the duck race winners

After each duck race:

1. Click **Duck race winners**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Choose **Boxing Day** or **Carnival** and set the date of the race.
3. Under **Prizes**, add one line per winning duck: the prize (1st, 2nd, Runner-up, Last duck home), the duck number, and each winner's name on its own line. Add the prize money if you want it shown.
4. If you have a photo of the printed results sheet, add it.
5. Press **Save**. The winners appear at the top of the Duck race winners page.

Only put names on the website if the winners are happy for them to appear.

## Our supporters

Click **Our supporters** to thank a business or organisation that helps. Fill in their name and one sentence about what they help with; add their website and logo only if they're happy for you to. Untick **Current supporter** when they stop, and they move to the "past" list.

## Add a year's Wolf's Pit results

1. Get the results as a spreadsheet. Make sure the first row is exactly these five headings: **position, name, club, category, time**, with times written like **0:41:23**. Leave club or category empty if you don't have them.
2. Save it as a **CSV** file (in Excel: File → Save As → CSV).
3. Click **Wolf's Pit results**, then **Add an entry**.
4. Fill in the **Year** and **Race date**, and upload the CSV under **Results file**. If you have the original PDF, upload that too.
5. Press **Save**. The results, podium and search update by themselves.

If the results don't appear after 5 minutes, the file probably isn't quite right (often the headings or the time format). Tell Peter.

## Privacy notice

Click **Privacy notice** to change its wording. Set **Last checked** to today's date when the committee has approved it. The part about the contact form is added automatically once the form is switched on.

## Dates for your diary, totals and contact details

Open **Site settings**:

- **Dates for your diary:** add upcoming public dates. Past ones disappear by themselves.
- **Total raised** and **Number of groups helped:** once these are filled in, they appear on the homepage and the "Where the money goes" page.
- **Contact email**, **When we meet**, donation buttons and so on.

Anything still in [SQUARE BRACKETS] is hidden on the website until it's filled in.

## Meet the dads

Open **Meet the dads**. Only add someone **once they've agreed** to appear on the site. Their card appears as soon as their real name replaces "[MEMBER NAME]".

## If something goes wrong

- **Nothing changed after 5 minutes:** something in the entry wasn't accepted, often a missing required box such as a photo description, or a results file in the wrong format. The website stays as it was, so nothing breaks. Check the entry, or ask Peter.
- **Made a mistake:** just edit it again and save. Every change is recorded, so anything can be undone. Ask Peter if you need an old version back.
- **Deleting:** please ask Peter before deleting anything.

## Asking for support and duck race winners

- **Asking for support (groups)** in the editor holds the "Does your group need support?" wording on the Where the money goes page. Fill in the committee's rules (who can ask, typical amounts, how and when requests are decided) and delete the `[PLACEHOLDER]` line.
- When you add a race in **Duck race winners**, the homepage shows "The winners are in!" for two weeks after the race date, then hides it by itself.

## Facebook posts arrive as draft news stories

Every morning the website checks the Bradda Dads Facebook page. Each new post becomes a **draft** story in the editor's **News** list (the Draft column says so). Drafts are not on the website.

To publish one:

1. Open it in **News**.
2. Check the **Headline** (it's made from the post's first line, so it may need tidying: no shouting, no emoji), the **Topic** (a guess) and the **Summary**.
3. If the photos are good, describe them: the descriptions start as "Photo 1 of 3 from the Facebook post…".
4. Untick **Draft** and save. It appears on the site after the next rebuild (a few minutes).

Not everything needs to be on the website: quick reminders ("1 hour to go!") and reposts can simply be **deleted**. A deleted draft never comes back, and its photos are tidied away automatically.

Things to watch for: children's names (never publish them), private details such as phone numbers, and photos someone might not want kept online.

**Photos from Facebook:** when you publish a Facebook story with three or more photos, the next update also turns its photos into an album on the Photos page (with the story's topic and date), and the story shows a "See all photos" strip. You can then rename the album or describe the photos under **Photo albums** in the editor.
