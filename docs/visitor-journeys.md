# Visitor journeys: improvements needed

From the visitor journeys review (26 Nov 2026). Most visitors are villagers on phones, arriving from a Facebook post, a poster or the Bradwell News. Runners and former residents mostly arrive from Google, landing on results pages and old albums.

✅ = done · ⏳ = needs information from Peter or the committee · 🔧 = can be built

## 1. Contact (the biggest blocker: 7 of 13 visitor groups need it)

- ⏳🔧 **Contact email and message form.** At the moment Facebook is the only way to get in touch, which shuts out older people, businesses, funders, and anyone wanting a photo removed privately. Needs a group email address and a Formspree account. **The group already has an email address, but Peter can't get into it (26 Nov 2026)**: find out who holds the login, or recover it, then use it for Formspree too. The form is already built and switches on once `contactFormUrl` is set in site.yaml.
- 🔧 Once there's an email, add it to: the privacy notice ("ask us to remove a photo"), the business card on Donate, the "Ask us for support" section, and the footer.
- ⏳ **A lunch booking route besides the newsletter slip**, such as a phone number or email for bookings and for arranging lifts or meals at home.

## 2. Event details: the site knows *when* but not *what, where and how much*

Every event page should answer: date, start time, place (postcode or map link), price, how to book or enter and by when, parking and access, what to bring, whether it suits children, and who to ask.

- ⏳ **Pensioners' lunch:** time, venue, how else to book. Also check the newsletter's name: is it "Bradda News" or "Bradwell News"?
- ⏳ **Duck race:** entry price (on the button too), start time, start point, raft race age groups.
- ⏳ **Bonfire:** gate time, display time, food, prices.
- ⏳ **Family Fun Day:** date, place, what's on, costs.
- ⏳ **Wolf's Pit 2027:** date and entry link.
- ⏳ **Christmas card:** where to drop off paper slips. Messages can only reach us via a SumUp note; that needs the form service.

## 3. Local groups asking for money

- ⏳ More funded examples on the map (e.g. school leavers' hoodies, once checked), plus real pin positions.
